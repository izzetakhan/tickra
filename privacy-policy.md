# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Introduction

Thank you for using Tickra.

Tickra is developed by **Fethra Labs**. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

Tickra does not require users to create an account.

The app may collect limited technical information automatically through third-party services for app functionality and advertising.

This may include:

- Device information
- Advertising identifiers
- Crash diagnostics (if enabled by the operating system)
- Anonymous usage information

## Advertising

Tickra displays advertisements using **Google AdMob**.

Google may collect and process information according to its own Privacy Policy.

Learn more:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Local Data

Workout settings, timer preferences, wallpapers and reminder settings are stored locally on your device.

Tickra does not upload this information to our servers.

## Permissions

Tickra may request access to:

- Notifications
- Vibration
- Storage (only when selecting wallpapers)

These permissions are used solely for app functionality.

## Children's Privacy

Tickra is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Updates will be published on this page.
