## Frequently Asked Questions

### Ads are not loading

Please make sure your internet connection is active.

### Notifications are not working

Check your device notification settings and ensure notifications are enabled for Tickra.

### Timer is not running in the background

Some Android manufacturers restrict background processes to save battery. Please exclude Tickra from battery optimization if necessary.
